PRAXIS Phase 30 chapter interpretation

Phase 30 evaluated whether the Phase 17 winning configuration, default_c2_p1, remained lower-confidence than baseline_default_off across multiple detector families.

The result was mixed but useful. Tree-based detectors showed the clearest confidence reduction. Random forest reduced mean proxy probability from 0.962200 for baseline_default_off to 0.580344 for default_c2_p1, a delta of -0.381856. Extra trees reduced mean proxy probability from 0.994749 to 0.838589, a delta of -0.156160. The calibrated linear SVC showed a small reduction, while gradient boosting showed only a negligible reduction. Logistic regression did not support the reduction and slightly increased confidence for default_c2_p1.

This means PRAXIS should not claim universal detector robustness. The correct claim is detector-family-dependent confidence reduction, strongest under non-linear tree-based detectors. Binary detection at the 0.50 threshold remained positive for nearly all configurations, so Phase 30 still supports confidence reduction rather than complete detector bypass.

This strengthens the dissertation because it shows that the controller result was not limited to one detector, while also honestly identifying detector-family limitations.
